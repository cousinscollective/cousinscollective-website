About Us

Built on the trust
that only family carries.

Cousins Collective was founded by Jessie Forbes and Lauren Bazan, cousins who share more than a family bond. They share a standard: that the professionals who build things worth building deserve support that actually rises to meet them.

We built Cousins Collective because we watched brilliant attorneys, consultants, and founders spend their most valuable hours on work that was never supposed to be theirs. The operations. The follow-up. The inbox. The calendar. The things that, if handled well, unlock everything.

We are Texas-based, US-only, retainer-driven, and deeply invested in the long game of every client we take on. We don't complete tasks. We become indispensable.
