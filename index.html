About Us

Built on the trust

Cousins Collective was founded by Jessie Forbes and Lauren Bazan, cousins who share more than a family bond. They share a standard: that the professionals who build things worth building deserve support that actually rises to meet them.

We built Cousins Collective because we watched brilliant consultants, founders, and business owners spend their most valuable hours on work that was never supposed to be theirs: managing inboxes, chasing follow up, coordinating schedules, and handling the coordination that pulls leaders away from the work only they can do.

We are based in Texas, serve clients nationally, work exclusively on retainer, and are deeply invested in the long game of every client we take on. We create the operational rhythm that helps your business run without everything depending on you.

FAQ

Common questions
answered directly.

We know you have questions before committing. Here are the ones we hear most often.

Are you virtual assistants?
We offer retainer-based executive operations, client coordination, and project support for professionals who need a consistent operational partner — not a task-by-task service. The work is broader, more proactive, and more structured than traditional virtual assistant support.
Do you work hourly?
Our services are retainer-based so clients have consistent, predictable support and a clear monthly scope. This structure allows us to operate proactively rather than reactively, which is what makes the support genuinely useful.
Who do you work best with?
Consultants, founders, private practice owners, executives, and service-based professionals who are excellent at their core work and need a trusted partner to handle the operational layer around it. Our clients typically have more to manage than one person can comfortably handle alone.
Can you manage my inbox and calendar?
Yes. Inbox and calendar management are core to our Executive Operations service and available in all retainer tiers. This includes triage, scheduling, response drafting, follow-up tracking, and weekly priority summaries — structured around how you actually work.
Do you work outside Texas?
Yes. Cousins Collective is Texas-based and serves clients nationally. All of our support is delivered remotely and is fully location-independent.
How quickly can we get started?
Most clients move from consultation to active support within 7 days. After your discovery call we deliver a custom proposal within 48 hours, and structured onboarding begins from there.
